## Preserved Figure Captions from the Paper

2. Figure 2: Mean number of novel appropriate responses by time and condition.
3. Figure 3: Mean appropriate novel responses by condition and time.
4. Figure 4: Mean number of high-quality and high-quality novel analogies by condition.

## Interpretation Caveat

The figures visualize laboratory task scores, not long-term creativity or organizational output. Use them to support the narrower claim that walking increased short-term divergent ideation in the tested conditions.
